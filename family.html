Day Four · Saturday, August 8
Day trip to Margate
Day Trip
10:20 AM
Train to the seaside
The fast train from St Pancras down to Margate on the Kent coast, arriving around noon.
Midday
TEARS at TKE Studios
Tracey Emin's studios in Margate, and the final show of the residency there.
Afternoon
Lunch on the seafront, and a look at Turner Contemporary
Pizza by the sea, and the gallery on the front if we fancy it.
Evening
Train back to London
Home to the hotel for a quiet dinner after a day out.
Day Five · Sunday, August 9
Hampstead & Kensington
Easy Pace
Late morning
The Freud Museum
Freud's house in Hampstead, kept as he left it - his study and the famous couch.
Midday
Kenwood House, on Hampstead Heath
A grand house at the top of the Heath, free to visit, with works by Rembrandt and Vermeer inside and a lovely view over London.
Afternoon
Lunch in Kensington, then Cecily Brown
Fresh pasta, then the Cecily Brown exhibition at the Serpentine gallery in Kensington Gardens.
Evening
Last dinner at the hotel
A good final night, and an early one - Monday starts before dawn.
